# noted — store listing (English)

**Title:** noted — notes & reminders

**Short description (≤80):**
Colourful notes, tickable lists, and reminders by time or place — on watch & web.

**Full description:**
noted is one place for everything on your mind: colourful notes in a stack that unfolds at a touch, shopping lists you tick off with a tap, and reminders that ring right on time — or right when you arrive.

✦ An editor that's a pleasure to write in — headings, lists, checkboxes, bold and colour
✦ Time reminders: repeating, per list line, snooze, and reminders that stay in the shade until you remove them
✦ Place reminders: "when I get to the supermarket", "when I leave home"
✦ Photos, voice notes with on-device transcription, drawings, links and document scanning
✦ Categories and subcategories, pinned notes, archive and trash
✦ Share a note or a whole folder by link — see who ticked what
✦ Sync across devices, a full website on your computer, and a display mode for your TV
✦ A Wear OS app and a home-screen widget
✦ Blur or lock notes with a PIN or fingerprint
✦ No account needed — then everything stays on your phone. No ads.

**Category:** Productivity
